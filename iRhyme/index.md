**One rhyme pair at a time.**

iRhyme (爱声律启蒙 / 愛聲律啟蒙) is an **offline sound-rhyme studio** — not another couplet dump. **No account required.** All **312** passages ship on your device: *Shenglü Qimeng*, *Li Weng Dui Yun* across **thirty rhymes**, *Qian Jia Shi*, and *You Xue Qiong Lin*. **Parallelism visualization** shows 对仗 instead of only printing it. **Same-rhyme compare** lines up two primers in one rhyme class. Start from **Today’s Rhyme**. **Recite** with paragraph highlighting. Practice in **Rhyme Academy**. Export **rhyme cards**. Add **widgets**, optional **Reading Live Activity**, **Siri & Shortcuts**, and optional **iCloud**. Originals are public-domain; notes and glossary are study aids. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

**At a glance:** 312 passages · 30 rhyme chapters · 127 authors · 120 famous lines · 93 glossary terms · parallelism view · same-rhyme compare · recite · Rhyme Academy · Rhyme Hunt · Today’s Rhyme · rhyme cards · widgets · Control Center · `irhyme://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started (step by step)

### 1. First launch

1. The App Store listing is being prepared. After release, install from the App Store and open **iRhyme**.
2. Complete the welcome guide: Today’s Rhyme → recitation → parallelism → Rhyme Academy → same-rhyme compare → gestures → privacy.
3. Replay anytime in **Settings → Help → Feature Guide** (`irhyme://feature-guide`).
4. Usage, widget, and shortcuts guides live under **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s rhyme on Home

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. The **Today’s Rhyme** hero shows today’s passage; tap to open detail.
3. Optional: enable local **Today’s Rhyme notifications** in Settings.
4. Jump from Home into recite, Rhyme Academy, Rhyme Hunt, favorites, and search.

### 3. Browse & search

1. Browse *Shenglü Qimeng*, *Li Weng Dui Yun* (thirty rhymes), *Qian Jia Shi*, *You Xue Qiong Lin*, rhyme classes, glossary, and **120 famous lines**.
2. Search titles, source text, and famous lines. On iOS, optional **handwriting search** recognizes characters on device.
3. Star passages into **Favorites** and gather them into personal **lists** (韵单).

### 4. Close reading, parallelism & rhyme cards

1. Switch **bilingual / Chinese only / English only**; toggle vernacular, English, and pinyin independently.
2. Open **parallelism visualization** and **same-rhyme compare** (`irhyme://compare?rhyme=dong`). Glossary: `irhyme://glossary`.
3. Try immersive reading, vertical layout, centering, font scale, and segmented pages.
4. Open **Rhyme Card** → pick style / ratio → save to Photos (add-only) or system share.

### 5. Recite & Rhyme Academy

1. Open the recitation workbench for highlighted TTS, adjustable voice and pace, optional vernacular / English captions, continuous play, and background audio.
2. **Rhyme Academy** (`irhyme://study`): fade, fill-blank, self-check, quiz, and pairing (`irhyme://pair`), with mastery tracking.
3. **Rhyme Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-couplet game.

### 6. Widgets, Siri & system features

1. Add **Today’s Rhyme / Famous Line / Favorites / Study Progress** widgets.
2. Use Control Center, Siri, Spotlight, and `irhyme://` deep links.
3. Optional Reading Live Activity stays available on supported iPhone devices.

### 7. Privacy & sync

1. Library, reading, recite (system TTS), Rhyme Academy, widgets, and local notifications work **fully offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud Sync** mirrors favorites, lists, history, progress, streaks, and notes through **your private iCloud**.
3. Tip the developer (optional StoreKit) never unlocks a paywall and never creates an account.
