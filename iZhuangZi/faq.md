<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library, vernacular, English, and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Dao Study, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud. **Ask Zhuangzi** uses on-device Apple Intelligence when available and does not require an iHTC Studio network.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

iZhuangZi is a **Zhuangzi studio**: bilingual close reading of all **33** chapters, **20 parables**, concept cards, on-device **Ask Zhuangzi**, karaoke-style **recite**, **Dao Study**, **Zhuang Hunt**, **Daily Zhuangzi**, 逍遥 cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump of *Xiaoyaoyou*.

### What texts are included?

The complete *Zhuangzi* in **33** chapters:

- Inner chapters **7** · Outer **15** · Miscellaneous **11**
- **701** paragraphs with vernacular Chinese and **James Legge (1891)** English
- **20** famous allegories · **90** famous lines

Chinese text follows a public-domain recension; Legge English is a study alignment, not a new translation claim.

Originals are public-domain. Vernacular, English, and notes are curated study aids, not academic editions.

### What is Ask Zhuangzi?

An optional on-device guide (`izhuangzi://ask`) using Apple Intelligence **Foundation Models** on iOS 26+ / macOS 26+ when available. Questions and passage context stay on the device. If the model is unavailable, bundled commentary still opens.


### What study modes are available?

**Fade**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, and **quiz**, with mastery tracking. **Zhuang Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game.

### What is Daily Zhuangzi?

A daily passage on Home, optionally **contextual**, with optional **local** reminders. Widgets can keep it on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists (庄单)**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Not yet — the listing is being prepared. We will add an App Store link on this page when it goes live. Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。语料、白话、英译与学习辅助均内置。安装后浏览、对照品读、诵读（系统 TTS）、庄学学堂、小组件与每日通知均可**离线**使用。可选例外：**StoreKit 打赏**（Apple）、分享到你选择的其他 App，以及可选 iCloud。**问庄子**在可用时使用本机 Apple Intelligence，不经过 iHTC Studio 网络。

### 和普通电子书有什么不同？

爱庄子是**庄学工作台**：三十三篇中英对照、**20 则寓言**、概念卡、本机**问庄子**、卡拉 OK 式**诵读**、**庄学学堂**、**寻庄**、**今日一子**、逍遥卡、小组件、可选实时活动、Siri 与快捷指令——不是静态《逍遥游》。

### 收录了哪些内容？

全本《庄子》**33** 篇：

- 内篇 **7** · 外篇 **15** · 杂篇 **11**
- **701** 段白话与 **James Legge（1891）** 英译
- **20** 则著名寓言 · **90** 条金句

中文取公版篇目；Legge 英译为学习对齐，并非新译主张。

原文为公版典籍。白话、英译与注解为学习参考，并非学术校勘本。

### 「问庄子」是什么？

可选的本机导读（`izhuangzi://ask`），在 iOS 26+ / macOS 26+ 且 Apple Intelligence 可用时使用 **Foundation Models**。问题与篇目上下文留在设备上。模型不可用时，仍可打开内置题解／赏析。


### 有哪些学堂模式？

**渐隐**、**填空**、**默写**与**测验**，并记录掌握度。首页 **寻庄** 是独立的找篇小游戏。

### 「今日一子」是什么？

首页每日一篇，可按情境挑选，并可开启**本地**提醒。小组件可把它留在主屏幕、锁定屏幕与待机。

### iCloud 同步包含什么？

可选同步 **收藏**、**庄单**、**笔记**、**阅读历史**、**学堂进度**与**打卡**，走你的私有 iCloud 键值存储。

### 你们收集我的数据吗？

**不收集。** 详见[隐私政策](privacy)。

### App Store Connect 的隐私政策地址怎么填？

打开本站 [隐私政策](privacy) 页，从浏览器地址栏复制当前网址。
