<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. All **957** surnames and stroke-order data ship on device. After install, browsing, detail reading, recite (system TTS), Study Hall, naming inspiration, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), optional system translation, and sharing to another app you choose.

### How is this different from a simple surname list?

iBaiJia is a **culture studio**: origins, halls, migration, distribution, **eight browse axes**, four-character **recitation**, **Study Hall** (recognition / pinyin / flashcards / chain / source quiz), **Daily Surname**, stroke tracing, share cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static table.

### How many surnames are included?

**957** surnames: **504** in traditional order plus **453** extended entries (**77** compound surnames). English glosses and cultural fields are bundled offline.

### What browse dimensions are available?

Pinyin, strokes, wuxing, single vs compound, traditional vs extended, surname source, population rank, and the full four-character *Hundred Family Surnames* text.

### What does recitation mean here?

The classic four-character original text in a dedicated studio: four-column layout, line highlight, tap-to-start, single-line loop, optional background audio and Live Activity.

### What study modes are available?

**Recognition quiz**, **Pinyin match**, **Flashcards**, **Four-character chain**, and **Source quiz**. High scores are stored locally. Naming inspiration runs entirely on device.

### What is Daily Surname?

A daily recommendation on Home with optional **local** reminders. Set **My Surname** for personalized shortcuts and widgets.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **surname lists**, **notes**, **study progress**, **streaks**, and related preferences via your private iCloud KVS.

### Can I back up my data?

Yes. Settings supports **plain-text export / import** (marked sections) for my surname, favorites, lists, notes, history, study scores, and more — still on your devices only.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796013615). Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

是的。全量 **957** 个姓与笔顺数据均内置。安装后浏览、详情阅读、四言诵读（系统 TTS）、姓氏学堂、取名灵感、小组件与每日通知均可**离线**使用。可选例外：**StoreKit 打赏**（Apple）、系统翻译，以及分享到你选择的其他 App。

### 和简单姓氏列表有什么不同？

爱百家姓是**文化工作台**：起源郡望、迁徙分布、**八维浏览**、四言**诵读**、**姓氏学堂**（认姓/拼音/闪卡/接龙/来源测验）、**今日一姓**、笔顺描红、分享卡、小组件、可选实时活动、Siri 与快捷指令——不是静态表格。

### 包含多少姓氏？

**957** 个姓：传统顺序 **504** + 扩展 **453**（含复姓 **77**）。英文释义与文化字段均离线内置。

### 有哪些浏览维度？

拼音、笔画、五行、单复姓、传统·扩展、得姓来源、人口排名，以及《百家姓》四言全文。

### 「诵读」指什么？

四言原文专用工作台：四列排版、句级高亮、点句起读、单句循环，可选后台音频与朗读实时活动。

### 学堂有哪些模式？

**认姓测验**、**拼音配对**、**闪卡复习**、**四言接龙**、**来源测验**。最高分本地保存。取名灵感纯本机运行。

### 什么是「今日一姓」？

首页每日推荐，可开**本地**提醒。设置**我的姓氏**可获得个性化入口与小组件。

### iCloud 同步包含什么？

可选同步**收藏**、**姓氏册**、**笔记**、**学习进度**、**打卡**等偏好，经你的私有 iCloud KVS。

### 可以备份数据吗？

可以。设置内支持**纯文本导出 / 导入**（分段标记），覆盖我的姓、收藏、姓氏册、笔记、历史、学堂分数等——仍在你的设备间。

### 你们收集数据吗？

**不收集。** 见 [隐私政策](privacy)：爱火腿肠工作室不收集数据；无广告/分析 SDK；无需账号。

### App Store Connect 隐私政策 URL 填什么？

在浏览器打开本站 [隐私政策](privacy) 页，从地址栏复制当前网址。

### 上架了吗？

已上架 — [App Store 下载](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796013615)。联系 [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) 获取支持。

### 系统要求？

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**，Mac **macOS 14.0+**。

### 如何联系支持？

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com)，通常 48 小时内回复。
