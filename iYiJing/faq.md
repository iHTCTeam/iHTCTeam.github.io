<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is this a fortune-telling app?

**No.** iYijing is an **educational Zhouyi study studio**. Cultural casting (coin / yarrow) teaches traditional procedure and symbolism with clear disclaimers — results are **not** predictions, advice, or decision engines.

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. All **64** hexagrams, Ten Wings, trigrams, and famous lines are bundled. After install, browsing, reading, recite (system TTS), casting, Study Hall, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), and sharing to another app you choose.

### How is this different from a simple I Ching reader?

iYijing is a **studio**: bilingual reading with English-display modes, relation maps, immersive layout, **cultural casting**, **Yijing Study** (six quiz modes + keyword hunt), wrong-book & mastery, Daily Hexagram, study cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static ebook or oracle feed.

### How much content is included?

**64** hexagrams, **5** Ten Wings texts, **8** trigrams, and **100** curated famous lines.

### What study modes are available?

Six quiz modes — **name / glyph / sequence / judgment fill-in / line position / trigrams** — plus **keyword hunt**. Wrong answers go to a wrong-book; mastery advances with streaks.

### What is Daily Hexagram?

A daily recommendation from a contextual pool. Optional **local** reminders. Widgets can show today’s gua too.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **hexagram lists**, **reading history**, **mastery**, **notes**, **casting records**, **wrong-book**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud with merge rules.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site’s [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser’s address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796014759). Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 13.0+**.
