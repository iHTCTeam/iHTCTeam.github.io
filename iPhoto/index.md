<section lang="en" markdown="1">

**iPhoto (爱修图)** is a privacy-first **local photo studio** for iPhone and iPad. Capture with full-frame live beauty, edit non-destructively, strip metadata before you share — **no account, no photo upload, no ads, no tracking.**

**At a glance:** Library · optional Capture · Settings · Search (iOS 18+) · 55 filters · one-tap enhance · crop straighten · side-by-side compare · stickers & frames · privacy export · Home & Lock widgets · 6 app icons · Siri, Shortcuts & `iphoto://` · optional iCloud preferences sync.

> iPhoto is an independent app and is **not affiliated with Apple Inc.**

---

## Part 1 — First Launch & Tabs

1. Download from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6787360978) and open **iPhoto**.
2. Complete the short **welcome guide** (replay later in **Settings → Feature Guide**).
3. When prompted, allow **Camera** (for Capture) and **Photos** (for import / optional save).
4. Choose a **launch tab** at the end of the guide, or later in Settings: **Capture** or **Library**.

No sign-in — you are ready immediately.

| Tab | What it does |
|-----|----------------|
| **Library** | Import, browse, search, multi-select, open the editor |
| **Capture** | Live camera with beauty & filters — hide this tab in Settings if unused |
| **Settings** | Appearance, icons, capture, export, guides |
| **Search** | iOS 18+ — search projects, filters, and editor tools |

---

## Part 2 — Library

- Import from **Photos**, or shoot in Capture.
- Switch **grid / list**; adjust columns in Settings.
- Filter by **All · Edited · Captured · Imported · Favorites**; sort by newest, oldest, or name.
- **Select** for batch share, save to Photos, favorite, or delete.
- Tap any item to open the non-destructive editor. **Original files stay** for imported projects.

---

## Part 3 — Capture with Live Beauty

1. Open **Capture** (or enable it in Settings if hidden).
2. Toggle **Beauty**. Fine-tune **Amount** plus Smooth, Whiten, Slim Face, Eyes, Teeth, Blush, Lipstick.
3. Optional: flash, countdown, grid, front mirror, live **filters** (favorite them; intensity slider).
4. When beauty or a filter is on, the preview shows the **full processed frame** — not just a face overlay.
5. Tap the shutter. The look is **baked once** into the saved original so the editor will not double-apply it.
6. Settings: remember beauty recipe, JPEG/HEVC, keep an unbaked negative, and what happens after a shot.

---

## Part 4 — Non-Destructive Editor

Open any project. Tools along the bottom:

| Tool | What you can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Adjust** | Exposure, contrast, white balance, and more — plus **one-tap enhance** (on-device) |
| **Filters** | **55** looks in 10 families, with intensity and favorites |
| **Beauty** | Presets + per-face controls when multiple faces are found |
| **Crop** | Ratios, rotate, flip, and **straighten (−45°…45°)** |
| **Effects** | One-tap styles (Soft / Film / Bold) and background blur |
| **Frames** | ~30 frames in five groups; thickness and a 10-color matte |
| **Stickers** | 51 stickers across expression, shape, mark, and season |
| **Text** | Captions that render into the final image |
| **Privacy** | Strip EXIF, GPS, and device metadata |

- **Tap** the preview for a side-by-side **Original | After** compare; **hold** to flash the original.
- Beauty uses **on-device Vision** — landmarks never leave your phone.
- Choose **All faces** or a single face to dial parameters independently.
- Optional: **one-tap enhance includes light beauty** (smooth + cool-brighten only — Settings).

---

## Part 5 — Export & Privacy

1. Tap export and pick **Original / High / Medium / Low**.
2. Optionally **Save to Photos**.
3. Enable **Confirm before export** if you want a safety check.
4. Turn on **Strip Metadata** before sharing travel photos or portraits.

Photos stay in the app sandbox (and optional Photos album) — **never uploaded** by iPhoto for analysis.

**爱修图（iPhoto）** 是面向 iPhone 与 iPad 的**隐私优先本地照片工作室**。拍摄即可整帧美颜，非破坏性修图，分享前可清除元数据——**无需账号、不上传原图、无广告、无追踪。**

**一眼看懂：** 资源库 · 可选拍摄 · 设置 · 搜索（iOS 18+）· 55 款滤镜 · 一键优化 · 裁剪拉直 · 并排对比 · 贴纸边框 · 隐私导出 · 主屏与锁屏小组件 · 6 套图标 · Siri、快捷指令与 `iphoto://` · 可选 iCloud 偏好同步。

> 爱修图为独立第三方应用，**与 Apple Inc. 无关联。**

## 一、首次打开与标签

1. 从 [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6787360978) 下载并打开 **爱修图**。
2. 完成简短**欢迎引导**（之后可在 **设置 → 功能引导** 重看）。
3. 按提示授予**相机**（拍摄）与**相册**（导入 / 可选保存）权限。
4. 可在引导末页或设置中选择**启动标签**：**拍摄** 或 **资源库**。

没有登录页——装好就能用。

| 标签 | 作用 |
|------|------|
| **资源库** | 导入、浏览、搜索、多选，进入编辑器 |
| **拍摄** | 实时美颜与滤镜相机；不用可在设置中隐藏 |
| **设置** | 外观、图标、拍摄、导出、指南 |
| **搜索** | iOS 18+ — 搜项目、滤镜与编辑工具 |

---

## 二、资源库

- 从系统**相册**导入，或在拍摄页直接拍。
- **网格 / 列表**可切换；列数在设置中调整。
- 按 **全部 · 已编辑 · 拍摄 · 导入 · 收藏** 筛选；按最新、最早或名称排序。
- **选择**后可批量分享、存相册、收藏或删除。
- 点开即修图。导入项目的**原图会保留**，编辑以参数保存。

---

## 三、拍摄与实时美颜

1. 打开**拍摄**（若已隐藏，先在设置中开启）。
2. 开启**美颜**，调节**总强度**与细项：磨皮、美白、瘦脸、大眼、美牙、腮红、唇彩。
3. 可选：闪光灯、倒计时、网格、前置镜像、实时**滤镜**（可收藏，带强度）。
4. 开启美颜或滤镜时，预览为**整帧处理后的画面**，不再只叠在脸部。
5. 按下快门，效果**一次烘焙**进库原图，编辑器不会再次叠加。
6. 设置里可记住美颜配方、选择 JPEG/HEVC、保留未烘焙底片，以及拍后去向。

---

## 四、非破坏性编辑器

打开任一项目，底部工具：

| 工具 | 能做什么 |
|------|----------|
| **调节** | 曝光、对比、白平衡等，另有**一键优化**（端侧分析） |
| **滤镜** | **55** 款、10 类，可调强度、可收藏 |
| **美颜** | 预设；多人脸时可按脸单独调 |
| **裁剪** | 比例、旋转、翻转，以及**拉直（−45°…45°）** |
| **特效** | 一键风格（柔光 / 胶片 / 鲜明）与背景虚化 |
| **边框** | 约 30 款、五组；可调粗细与 10 色底色 |
| **贴纸** | 51 款：表情、形状、标记、季节 |
| **文字** | 参与成片渲染 |
| **隐私** | 去除 EXIF、定位与设备信息 |

- **点按**预览并排对比「原图 | 效果」；**按住**闪回原图。
- 美颜使用**端侧 Vision**，关键点不离开设备。
- 可选**全部脸**或单张脸独立调节。
- 设置可开**一键优化附带轻美颜**（仅轻磨皮 + 冷亮，不做瘦脸大眼）。

## 五、导出与隐私

1. 点导出，选择 **原图 / 高 / 中 / 低** 画质。
2. 可选**写入系统相册**。
3. 可开**导出前确认**。
4. 分享旅行照或人像前，建议打开**清除元数据**。

照片只留在 App 沙盒（以及你选择的系统相册）——爱修图**不会上传**照片去做分析。
