**One classic a day. Five classics for a lifetime.**

iWuJing (爱五经 / 愛五經) is an **offline Five Classics study desk** for serious close reading, not another plain text dump. **No account required.** All **819** passages of *Shijing*, *Shujing*, *Liji*, *Yijing*, and *Chunqiu with Zuo Tradition* ship on your device with **bilingual reading**, **vernacular**, **gloss**, **variants**, and immersive layouts. **Recite** with paragraph highlighting, train in **Classics Study**, keep contextual **Daily Classic** routines, export **classic cards**, and use **widgets**, **Control Center**, **Siri**, **Spotlight**, optional **Reading Live Activity**, and optional **iCloud**. Zhouyi is presented **strictly as classical study**, with **no divination or fortune-telling features**. Texts and notes are curated from publicly available online sources. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

## Getting Started

### 1. First launch

1. Install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6797013704) and open **iWuJing**.
2. Complete the welcome guide: brand → close reading → recite → study → Daily Classic → privacy.
3. Replay anytime in **Settings → Help → Feature Guide**.
4. In-app **Usage / Widget / Shortcuts** guides live under **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s classic on Home

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. The **Daily Classic** hero shows today’s passage; tap to open detail.
3. Optional: enable local **Daily Classic notifications** in Settings.
4. Continue reading, see famous lines, and jump to recite / study / hunt from the home shortcuts.

### 3. Browse & search

1. Open **Browse**.
2. Explore the Five Classics by collection, theme, famous lines, and authors.
3. Use **Search** for titles, body text, vernacular, and gloss terms.
4. Save passages to **Favorites**, build personal **lists** (经单), and batch-open a whole list for study or recitation.

### 4. Bilingual reading & classic cards

1. On detail, switch between **bilingual / Chinese only / English only**.
2. Toggle **pinyin**, **vernacular anthology**, and **English** independently.
3. Use immersive reading, segmented pages, wide-screen layouts, gloss lookup, and variant notes.
4. Open **Classic Card** to export a shareable image.

### 5. Recite & study

1. Tap **Recite** on detail, or open the recitation workbench directly.
2. Follow paragraph highlighting while listening.
3. Configure the **Playback Content matrix** in Settings.
4. Use **Classics Study** for recitation, fill-blank, self-check, quiz, and **Hunt**.

### 6. Widgets, Siri & system integration

1. Add **Daily Classic / Famous Line / Study Progress / Favorites** widgets.
2. Use **Control Center**, **Siri**, and `iwujing://` deep links for today, random, study, recite, favorites, and search.
3. Optional **Spotlight** indexing is available in Settings.
4. Optional **Reading Live Activity** appears on supported iPhone devices.

### 7. Privacy & sync

1. Core reading, recite, study, widgets, and notifications work **fully offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud Sync** mirrors favorites, lists, history, progress, streaks, and notes through **your private iCloud**.
3. Tip the developer (optional StoreKit) never unlocks paywalled content and never creates an account.

## Features

- **819 passages** across the Five Classics
- **Bilingual close reading** with vernacular and English
- **Gloss & variants** lookup
- **Recitation workbench** with Playback Content matrix
- **Classics Study** with mastery and Hunt
- **Daily Classic**, widgets, Control Center, Siri, Spotlight, and deep links
- **No account, no ads, no analytics SDK**

## 保姆级上手教程

### 1. 第一次打开

1. 从 [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6797013704) 安装并打开**爱五经**。
2. 跟随欢迎引导：品牌 → 对照品读 → 诵读 → 学堂 → 每日一经 → 隐私。
3. 之后可在 **设置 → 帮助 → 功能引导** 重播。
4. **设置 → 帮助** 内还有使用说明、小组件指南与快捷指南。

### 2. 首页看「每日一经」

1. 打开**首页**。
2. Hero 卡片展示今日篇目，点按进入详情。
3. 可在设置里开启**本地每日提醒**。
4. 首页还可快速进入名句、继续阅读、诵读、学堂与寻经。

### 3. 分类浏览与搜索

1. 打开**分类**。
2. 按五经书目、主题、作者、名句浏览。
3. 用**搜索**查标题、正文、白话与训诂词头。
4. 收藏篇目，整理个人**经单**，并对整个列表批量诵读或练习。

### 4. 对照品读与经卡

1. 详情页可切换**对照 / 仅中 / 仅英**。
2. 拼音、白话合集与英文可分别开关。
3. 支持沉浸阅读、分段阅读、训诂与异文查看。
4. 打开**经卡**导出分享图。

### 5. 诵读与经学学堂

1. 在详情点**诵读**，或直接进入诵读工作台。
2. 跟随段级高亮听读。
3. 在设置中配置**播放内容矩阵**。
4. 进入**经学学堂**做背诵、填空、自测、测验与**寻经**。

### 6. 小组件、Siri 与系统能力

1. 添加**每日一经 / 名句 / 学习进度 / 收藏**小组件。
2. 通过**控制中心**、**Siri** 与 `iwujing://` 深链打开今日、随机、诵读、学堂、收藏或搜索。
3. **Spotlight** 可在设置中按需开启。
4. 支持可选**品读实时活动**。

### 7. 数据、同步与隐私

1. 阅读、诵读、学堂、小组件与通知在安装后即可**全程离线**使用。
2. 可选 **iCloud 同步**会通过**你的私有 iCloud**同步收藏、经单、历史、进度、打卡与笔记。
3. 可选打赏不会解锁付费墙，也不会创建账号。
