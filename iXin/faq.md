<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library, vernacular, English, and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Heart School, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

iXin is a **Yangming studio**: bilingual close reading, a **Knowing-Acting journal**, **Three Questions of Liangzhi**, concept graph / timeline / map / people, karaoke-style **recite**, **Heart School**, **Heart Hunt**, **Daily Insight**, insight cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump of *Chuanxilu*.

### What texts are included?

**102** passages of Wang Yangming’s school:

- *Chuanxilu* (*Instructions for Practical Living*)
- *Inquiry on the Great Learning*
- *Instructions to Students at Longchang*
- *Essay on Burying Travelers*, plus poems, annals, concept cards, and selected letters

**8** authors in the circle · **100** famous lines. English follows the spirit of Henke (1916) as a study aid.

Originals are public-domain. Vernacular, English, and notes are curated study aids, not academic editions.

### What is the Knowing-Acting journal?

A daily **known / acted / reflect** log (`ixin://journal`) so 知行合一 is practiced, not only read. **Three Questions of Liangzhi** (`ixin://liangzhi`) is a short conscience prompt. Optional iCloud can mirror journal entries through **your** private KVS.


### What study modes are available?

**Fade**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, and **quiz**, with mastery tracking. **Heart Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game.

### What is Daily Insight?

A daily passage on Home, optionally **contextual**, with optional **local** reminders. Widgets can keep it on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists (心单)**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks**, and **Knowing-Acting journal** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Not yet — the listing is being prepared. We will add an App Store link on this page when it goes live. Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

- 《瘗旅文》，以及诗文、年谱、概念卡与书信精选
