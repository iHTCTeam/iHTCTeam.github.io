<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. All **174** passages are bundled. After install, browsing, reading, recite (system TTS), study, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), and sharing to another app you choose.

### How is this different from a simple classics reader?

iArtOfWar is a **studio**: **bilingual + vernacular reading**, karaoke-style **recite** with a playback content matrix, **Strategy classroom** (recitation / fill-blank / self-check / quiz / Hunt), **contextual Daily Stratagem**, share cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump of text.

### What texts are included?

**174** passages: *The Art of War*, *Sun Bin*, the *Wujing* classics (吴子 / 司马法 / 六韬 / 三略 / 尉缭子 / 李卫公问对), and the *Thirty-Six Stratagems*, plus **101** famous lines / stratagems and **9** authors.

### What does “recite” mean here?

Product term for read-aloud. Follow paragraph highlighting while listening. In Settings you can configure **what is spoken** (title / author / body × original / vernacular / English) independently from what you see. When a continuous queue finishes, you can jump into the Strategy classroom.

### What study modes are available?

**Recitation**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, **quiz**, plus **Hunt (寻谋)**. Mastery has levels with streaks. You can practice a single segment or a batch from favorites / strategy lists (兵单).

### What is the Daily Stratagem?

A recommendation based on **solar terms**, **lunar festivals**, and **seasons**, with same-day cache. Optional **local** reminders.

### Why do some Sun Bin chapters look incomplete?

Silver Sparrow Hill (*银雀山*) manuscripts are fragmentary. The app shows a one-time note at the top of the original text for those chapters; academic endnotes are not repeated under every verse.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **strategy lists**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTCTeam; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site’s [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser’s address bar.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 13.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。全部 **174** 篇内置。安装后，浏览、品鉴、诵读（系统 TTS）、学堂、小组件与本地每日通知都**不需要网络**。可选例外：打赏走 **Apple StoreKit**；以及你主动分享到其他 App。

### 和其他兵法 App 有什么不同？

爱兵法是**工作台**：**中英对照 + 白话**、可混读的**诵读**、**谋略学堂**（背诵 / 填空 / 自测 / 测验 / 寻谋）、**情境每日一计**、谋卡、小组件、可选实时活动、Siri 与快捷——不是静态文本列表。

### 收录了哪些书？

**174** 篇：孙子、孙膑、武经诸书（吴子 / 司马法 / 六韬 / 三略 / 尉缭子 / 李卫公问对）与三十六计，另有 **101** 条名句/名计与 **9** 位作者。

### 「诵读」是什么？

即朗读跟读。听读时跟随段级高亮。设置里可单独配置**播放内容矩阵**，与屏幕显示语言解耦。连续队列播完可转入谋略学堂。

### 学堂有哪些模式？

**背诵、填空、自测对照、测验**，另有**寻谋**。掌握度分级配合打卡。可练本段，也可从收藏 / 兵单批量练习。

### 「每日一计」是什么？

结合**节气 / 农历节日 / 季节**的本地推荐（同日缓存）。可选**本机**提醒。

### 为什么有的孙膑篇看起来不完整？

银雀山简本残缺。App 会在原文区顶部一次提示；不会在每段白话/英译下重复学术尾注。

### iCloud 同步什么？

可选同步**收藏、兵单、浏览记录、掌握度与打卡**，走你的私有 iCloud。

### 会收集我的数据吗？

**不会。** 见 [隐私政策](privacy)。

### App Store Connect 应填的隐私政策 URL？

打开本站 [隐私政策](privacy) 页，从浏览器地址栏复制网址。

### 系统要求？

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**，Mac **macOS 13.0+**。

### 如何联系支持？

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — 通常 48 小时内回复。
