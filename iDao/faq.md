<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. All **81** Tao Te Ching chapters (with vernacular, pinyin, dual commentaries, and dual English editions), Qingjing Jing, Yinfu Jing, and famous lines are bundled. After install, browsing, reading, recite (system TTS), Study Hall, Seek the Tao, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), and sharing to another app you choose.

### How is this different from a simple Tao Te Ching reader?

iDao is a **studio**: per-character **pinyin**, **two classical commentaries**, **two English editions** (switch or compare), karaoke-style **recite**, **Study Hall** (fade / fill-blank / self-check / quiz), **Seek the Tao**, **Daily Chapter**, Dao cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static ebook.

### How much content is included?

**81** chapters of the Tao Te Ching, plus Qingjing Jing and Yinfu Jing for further reading, and **74** bilingual famous lines.

### What does “recite” / “诵读” mean here?

Product term for read-aloud with **line-level highlighting**. In Settings you can configure **what is spoken** (original / vernacular / English) independently from what you see.

### What study modes are available?

**Fade**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, **quiz**, plus **Seek the Tao (寻道)**. Mastery has four badge levels. You can practice one chapter or a batch from favorites / lists (道单).

### What is Daily Chapter?

A daily recommendation drawn from a contextual pool (solar terms / festivals / seasons). Optional **local** reminders. Widgets can show today’s chapter too.

### Can I switch commentaries and English editions?

Yes. On chapter detail you can switch **Wang Bi ↔ Heshang Gong**, and choose or compare the two English editions when available. Qingjing / Yinfu may hide bilingual compare when an English edition is absent.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists**, **reading history**, **study progress / mastery**, **notes**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS with **bidirectional merge**.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site’s [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser’s address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796016006). Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。全部 **81** 章《道德经》（含白话、拼音、双家古注与双语英译），以及《清静经》《阴符经》与名句均内置。安装后，浏览、品读、诵读（系统 TTS）、道学堂、寻道、小组件与本地每日通知都**不需要网络**。可选例外：打赏走 **Apple StoreKit**；以及你主动分享到其他 App。

### 和其他道德经 App 有什么不同？

爱道德经是**工作台**：逐字**拼音**、**双家古注**、**两套英译**（可切换对照）、句级高亮**诵读**、**道学堂**（渐隐 / 填空 / 自测 / 测验）、**寻道**、**每日一章**、道卡、小组件、可选实时活动、Siri 与快捷——不是静态电子书。

### 收录了多少内容？

《道德经》**81** 章，另附《清静经》《阴符经》延伸阅读，以及 **74** 条双语名句。

### 「诵读」是什么意思？

指带**句级高亮**的朗读。可在设置里配置**播放内容矩阵**（原文 / 白话 / 英文），与屏幕显示解耦。

### 有哪些道学堂模式？

**渐隐、填空、自测对照、测验**，外加**寻道**。掌握度有四级徽章。可从收藏或道单批量练习。

### 「每日一章」是什么？

结合节气 / 节日 / 季节的情境推荐。可选**本机**提醒。小组件也可展示今日一章。

### 可以切换注解和英译吗？

可以。详情页可在**王弼注 ↔ 河上公章句**之间切换，并选择或对照两套英译（有译时）。清静 / 阴符若无英译会隐藏对照。

### iCloud 同步覆盖哪些内容？

可选同步：**收藏**、**道单**、**浏览记录**、**掌握度**、**笔记**与**打卡**——走你的私有 iCloud KVS，并做**双向合并**。

### 会收集我的数据吗？

**不会。** 见 [隐私政策](privacy)：iHTC Studio 不收集数据；无广告/分析 SDK；无账号。

### App Store Connect 填哪条隐私政策 URL？

打开本站的 [隐私政策](privacy) 页面，从浏览器地址栏复制当前网址即可。

### 已经上架了吗？

是的 — [在 App Store 下载](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796016006)。支持问题可联系 [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com)。

### 系统要求？

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**，Mac **macOS 14.0+**。
