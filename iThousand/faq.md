<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. All **122** passages and **1661** character cards ship on device. After install, browsing, reading, recite (system TTS), Primer Hall, Hunt, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), optional system translation, and sharing to another app you choose.

### How is this different from a simple classics reader?

iThousand is a **literacy studio**: **bilingual + vernacular + pinyin**, **stroke-order character cards**, karaoke-style **recite**, **Primer Hall** (recitation / fill-blank / self-check / quiz / Hunt), contextual **Daily Line**, share cards (千卡), five widget types, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump of text.

### What texts are included?

**122** passages: *Thousand Character Classic* (stanza groups), *San Zi Jing*, *Hundred Family Surnames*, and *Di Zi Gui*, plus **1661** character cards and **104** famous lines.

### What are character cards?

Tap a character in a passage to open sound, meaning, radical, stroke count, and **stroke-order animation**. Bookmark hard characters into **Saved Characters** and add the Saved Characters widget.

### What does “recite” mean here?

Product term for read-aloud. Follow line highlighting while listening. In Settings you can configure **what is spoken** (title / author / body × original / vernacular / English) independently from what you see.

### What study modes are available?

**Recitation**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, **quiz**, plus **Hunt (寻字)**. Mastery has levels with streaks. You can practice a single passage or a batch from favorites / lists (千单).

### What is the Daily Line?

A contextual recommendation (solar terms / lunar festivals / seasons) with same-day cache. Optional **local** reminders.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists**, **saved characters**, **famous-line favorites**, **reading history**, **study progress**, **streaks**, and **notes** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site’s [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser’s address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796010801). Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。全部 **122** 篇与 **1661** 张字卡内置。安装后，浏览、品读、诵读（系统 TTS）、蒙学堂、寻字、小组件与本地每日通知都**不需要网络**。可选例外：打赏走 **Apple StoreKit**；可选系统翻译；以及你主动分享到其他 App。

### 和其他国学 App 有什么不同？

爱千字文是**识字学堂**：**中英对照 + 白话 + 拼音**、**字卡笔顺**、可混读的**诵读**、**蒙学堂**（背诵 / 填空 / 自测 / 测验 / 寻字）、**情境每日一句**、千卡、五类小组件、可选实时活动、Siri 与快捷——不是静态文本列表。

### 收录了哪些书？

**122** 篇：《千字文》句组、《三字经》、《百家姓》与《弟子规》，另有 **1661** 张字卡与 **104** 条名句。

### 字卡是什么？

在篇目中点字，查看音义、部首、笔画，并播放**笔顺描红**。难字可收入**生字本**，并加上生字小组件。

### 「诵读」是什么？

即朗读跟读。听读时跟随句级高亮。设置里可单独配置**播放内容矩阵**，与屏幕显示语言解耦。

### 有哪些学堂模式？

**背诵 / 填空 / 自测 / 测验**，另有**寻字**。掌握度分级配合打卡；可练单篇，也可从收藏 / 千单批量练习。

### 「每日一句」是什么？

结合节气 / 农历节日 / 季节的每日推荐，同日缓存。可在设置中开启**本地**提醒。

### iCloud 同步包含什么？

可选同步：**收藏、千单、生字本、名句收藏、阅读记录、学堂进度、打卡、笔记**（你的私有 iCloud KVS）。

### 你们收集我的数据吗？

**不收集。** 见 [隐私政策](privacy)：爱火腿肠工作室不收集数据；无广告/分析 SDK；无账号。

### App Store Connect 的隐私政策 URL 填哪里？

打开本站 [隐私政策](privacy) 页，从浏览器地址栏复制当前网址。

已上架 — [App Store 下载](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796010801)。联系 [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) 获取支持。

### 系统要求？

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**，Mac **macOS 14.0+**。

### 如何联系支持？

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — 通常 48 小时内回复。
