iHTCTeam built iBible (爱圣经 / 愛聖經).

## Summary

**Data Not Collected.** iBible is designed so that **core Scripture reading works without sending your data to iHTCTeam**. **No account. No ads. No analytics SDK.** Bible text is **bundled on your device**. Optional **iCloud Sync** for favorites and verse lists uses **your private iCloud** only.

## Data Stored on Your Device

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Bible database | Bundled Scripture — 66 books, ~31,021 verses |
| Favorites & verse lists | Verses and lists you create |
| Highlights & reading state | Colors, last-read chapter, streak, plan progress |
| Preferences | Theme, accents, TTS, bilingual layout, notifications |
| Notification settings | Today’s Verse reminders (local scheduling only) |

## Network

After install, **everyday reading does not require the internet**. Opening App Store pages, sending email feedback, or completing an optional tip uses **Apple** or **your mail app** — not an iHTCTeam backend for Scripture.

## iCloud Sync

Optional sync for **favorites** and **verse lists** through your private iCloud key-value store. Scripture content itself is not uploaded to iHTCTeam servers.

## Permissions

| Permission | When | Why |
|------------|------|-----|
| Notifications | Today’s Verse reminder | Local scheduling only — no server |
| iCloud | Optional sync | Sync favorites and verse lists |
| Photos (add) | Save share cards | On-device images you choose to save |

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com)

iHTCTeam 开发了爱圣经。

## 摘要

**不收集数据。** 爱圣经的设计目标是：**核心读经无需向 iHTCTeam 发送你的数据**。**无账号、无广告、无分析 SDK。** 圣经文本**内置在设备本地**。可选 **iCloud 同步**收藏与经文单，仅使用**你的私有 iCloud**。

## 设备本地存储

| 数据 | 用途 |
|------|------|
| 圣经数据库 | 内置经文 — 66 卷、约 31,021 节 |
| 收藏与经文单 | 你创建的经文与列表 |
| 高亮与阅读状态 | 颜色、上次阅读、连续天数、计划进度 |
| 偏好设置 | 主题、强调色、朗读、双语布局、通知 |
| 通知设置 | 今日一经提醒（仅本地调度） |

## 网络

安装后，**日常阅读不需要联网**。打开 App Store 页面、发送反馈邮件或完成可选打赏时，会使用 **Apple** 或**你的邮件应用**——经文本身不经过 iHTCTeam 后端。

## iCloud 同步

可选通过私有 iCloud 键值存储同步**收藏**与**经文单**。圣经正文不会上传到 iHTCTeam 服务器。

## 权限

| 权限 | 时机 | 原因 |

| iCloud | 可选同步 | 同步收藏与经文单 |
| 照片（添加） | 保存分享卡片 | 仅保存你选择的本机图片 |
