**Give your mind a real reboot.**

iZen (爱禅) is an **offline three-layer recovery toolbox** for long-term mental fatigue — not another thin meditation player. **No account required.** Cut what drains you, rebuild what restores you, and lighten the root load with **47 practices**, **~500 quotes**, **17 science briefs**, a **multi-mode breathing desk**, **procedural soundscape mixer**, **1-minute stress assessment**, **crisis helplines**, and **Daily Zen**. Export **6-style share cards**, add **widgets**, optional **Breathing Live Activity**, **Siri & Shortcuts**, and optional **iCloud**. **No ads. No tracking.**

**At a glance:** Instant recovery · Daily rebuild · Root relief · 47 practices · ~500 quotes · 17 science articles · breathing (4-7-8 / box / physiological sigh…) · soundscape · journal · three tasks · cognitive reframe · stats · crisis directory · Daily Zen · share cards · widgets · Control Center · `izen://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

> You don’t have to “push through.” Lower the drain, raise the restore — one breath at a time.

## Getting Started (step by step)

### 1. First launch

1. Install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757993810) and open **iZen**.
2. Complete the **7-page welcome guide** (brand → three layers → breathing → tools → system extras → privacy).
3. Replay anytime: **Settings → Help → Feature Guide**.
4. In-app **Usage / Widget / Shortcuts** guides live under **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Start from Home

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. Read **Daily Zen** — today’s quote with optional reflection; tap to open detail or share a card.
3. Check the **recovery dashboard**: streak, recent sessions, assessment summary.
4. Quick actions: **Breathe** · **SOS** · **Soundscape** · **Continue last session**.
5. Optional: enable **Daily Zen notifications** in Settings (local only).

### 3. Browse the three layers

1. Open **Browse** (on by default; can be hidden in Settings).
2. Explore **Instant recovery**, **Daily rebuild**, and **Root relief**, plus **Quotes** and **Science** (five subcategories).
3. First visit shows layer guide cards (dismissible; reopen from the menu).
4. Switch **list / grid / cards**; filter sections; open practice, quote, or science detail.
5. Deep links like `izen://browse/instant` jump to a layer.

### 4. Breathe (breathing desk)

1. Open **Breathe** from Home, Control Center, widget, or `izen://breathe`.
2. Pick a mode: **4-7-8**, **box**, **physiological sigh**, resonance, and more.
3. Choose visual theme: **ink-wash** or **soft glow**; orb or box pattern; optional 3-second countdown.
4. Optional **voice guidance** for breath phases (can turn off); pre/post mood check; abandon mid-session needs confirmation; auto-save on finish.
5. Procedural audio + haptics; optional **Zen mode** minimal UI; optional **Live Activity** and **HealthKit Mindfulness minutes** (both off by default).

### 5. Soundscape, journal & tools

1. **Soundscape**: multi-track procedural mixer with scene presets — `izen://soundscape`.
2. **Journal**: structured mood log — `izen://journal`.
3. **1-minute assessment**: sleep / tasks / social drain → personalized recommendations — `izen://assessment`.
4. **Three tasks** board and **cognitive reframe** entries for root-layer work.
5. **Stats**: streak, pressure curve, session summaries — `izen://stats`.

### 6. Crisis support

1. Home shows a **local-region helpline preview** (from bundled data, not a live API).
2. Open the full **global searchable directory** — phone / SMS / web where available.
3. Medical disclaimer: iZen is **not** emergency care; call local emergency services when needed.

### 7. Favorites, search & share cards

1. Star practices and quotes; build **Zen lists** (custom collections); **batch-edit** favorites when cleaning up.
2. **Search** full text across practices, quotes, and science (iOS 18+ dedicated Search tab).
3. **Share cards**: 6 styles × 4 ratios; optional “today’s reflection” line; save to Photos or share.

### 8. Widgets, Siri & privacy

1. Add widgets: **Daily Zen**, **Quick Breathe**, **Recovery Streak**, **SOS Favorites**.
2. Control Center (iOS 18+): breathe, SOS, daily, journal, assessment, soundscape.
3. Configure Home Screen quick actions in **Settings → Shortcuts**.
4. Optional **iCloud** sync for favorites, logs, assessments, lists, streaks. **Spotlight off by default.**
