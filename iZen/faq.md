<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Do I need an account or the internet?

**No account.** After install, practices, quotes, science, breathing, soundscape, tools, widgets, and local notifications work **offline**. Optional exceptions: iCloud you enable, HealthKit mindfulness write, crisis web links you open, StoreKit tip, and mail feedback.

### How is this different from a meditation app?

iZen is a **three-layer recovery toolbox**: instant breathing + soundscape, daily rebuild tools (journal, habits), and root relief (three tasks, cognitive reframe), backed by **47 practices**, **~500 quotes**, and **17 science briefs** — not just one looping track.

### What are the three layers?

1. **Instant recovery** — breathe, SOS pack, grounding  
2. **Daily rebuild** — micro-habits, sleep rhythm, journal, movement & nutrition nudges  
3. **Root relief** — three daily tasks, boundaries, cognitive reframe, decision outsourcing  

Browse shows guide cards on first visit; deep links like `izen://browse/instant` jump to a layer.

### What breathing modes are included?

Data-driven modes include **4-7-8**, **box**, **physiological sigh**, resonance, and more. Visual themes: **ink-wash** or **soft glow**; orb or box pattern. Optional 3s countdown, pre/post mood, Live Activity, and HealthKit (both off by default).

### Is the soundscape downloaded audio?

**No large tracks.** Soundscape uses **procedural synthesis** mixed on device — rain, wind, tones, etc. — with scene presets.

### What is the 1-minute assessment?

A quick profile from **sleep**, **task load**, and **social drain** that feeds Home recommendations and opens the right practice desk.

### How do crisis helplines work?

Bundled `crisis_helplines.json` (30+ regions). Home previews your **system locale region**; the full directory is searchable globally. iZen is **not** emergency care — call local emergency services when needed.

### Does iCloud sync everything?

Optional iCloud KVS mirrors favorites, Zen lists, logs, assessments, streaks, and related progress. **No iHTC Studio backend.**

### Why doesn’t Live Activity or HealthKit work?

Both are **off by default**. Enable in Settings, start breathing in the **foreground**, and grant Health write if you want Mindfulness minutes.

### Is Spotlight on by default?

**No.** Turn on Spotlight indexing in Settings if you want passages searchable from the Home Screen.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site’s [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser’s address bar.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

### 需要账号或联网吗？

**无需账号。** 安装后，练习、语录、科学、呼吸、音景、工具、小组件与本地通知均可 **离线**。可选例外：你开启的 iCloud、HealthKit 正念写入、你主动打开的危机网页、StoreKit 打赏与邮件反馈。

### 和常见冥想 App 有什么不同？

爱禅是 **三层恢复工具箱**：即时呼吸 + 音景、日常重建（日志、微习惯）、根源减负（三件事、认知重构），并有 **47** 练习、**约 500** 语录、**17** 篇科学依据——不是单一循环音轨。

### 三层体系是什么？

1. **即时回血** — 呼吸、SOS 急救包、接地  
2. **日常重建** — 微习惯、睡眠节律、情绪日志、运动与饮食微习惯  
3. **根源减负** — 每日三件事、心理边界、认知重构、决策外包  

浏览首次有引导卡；`izen://browse/instant` 等深链可直达某层。

### 有哪些呼吸模式？

数据驱动模式含 **4-7-8**、**方形**、**生理叹息**、共鸣等。视觉：**水墨** 或 **柔光**；圆环或方形。可选 3 秒倒数、练前/练后情绪、实时活动与 HealthKit（均默认关）。

### 音景是下载的音频吗？

**不是大体积音轨。** 音景用 **程序化合成** 在本机混音——雨、风、 tone 等——带场景预设。

### 1 分钟自评是什么？

根据 **睡眠**、**任务量**、**人际消耗** 快速画像，用于首页推荐并直达对应练习工作台。

### 危机热线怎么用？

内置 `crisis_helplines.json`（30+ 地区）。首页预览 **系统地区**；完整目录全球可搜。爱禅 **不是** 紧急医疗服务——遇险请拨当地急救电话。

### iCloud 会同步全部吗？

可选 iCloud KVS 同步收藏、禅单、日志、自评、打卡等。**无 iHTC Studio 后端。**

### 为什么看不到实时活动或 HealthKit？

二者 **默认关闭**。在设置开启，于 **前台** 开始呼吸；若需正念分钟再授予健康写入权限。

### Spotlight 默认开启吗？

**否。** 若希望主屏幕可检索内容，请在设置中开启 Spotlight 索引。

### App Store Connect 隐私政策网址？

打开本站的 [隐私政策](privacy) 页面，从浏览器地址栏复制当前网址即可。

### 系统要求？

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**，Mac **macOS 14.0+**。

### 如何联系支持？

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com)——通常 48 小时内回复。
