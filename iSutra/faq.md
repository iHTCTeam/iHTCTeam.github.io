<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The **144-entry** index and bundled sutra data ship on device. After install, browsing, reading, recite (system TTS), study, chant, practice, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Copy-sutra strokes stay on device. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), and sharing to another app you choose.

### How is this different from a simple sutra reader?

iSutra is a **practice studio**: **bilingual + vernacular reading**, karaoke-style **recite**, **Study Hall** (recitation / fill-blank / self-check / quiz / Hunt), **wooden-fish chant**, **copy-sutra** (iOS), **morning / evening practice**, **Daily Sutra**, Dharma cards, widgets, optional Live Activities, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump of text.

### What texts are included?

A **144-entry** offline index covering frequently recited classics (Heart Sutra, Diamond Sutra in 32 divisions, Amitabha Sutra, Medicine Buddha vows, Universal Gate, Dizang in 13 chapters, selected mantras, Platform Sutra excerpts, Forty-Two Chapters, Eight Awakenings, and more), plus **101** famous lines. Content is for **learning and educational use**.

### What does “recite” mean here?

Product term for read-aloud (**诵经**). Follow paragraph highlighting while listening. In Settings you can configure **what is spoken** (original / vernacular / English) independently from what you see.

### What are chant, copy-sutra, and practice?

- **Chant** — on-device wooden-fish counter with daily / lifetime totals, goals, tones, and optional Chant Live Activity.
- **Copy-sutra (iOS)** — PencilKit tracing with per-line drafts and PNG / PDF export; strokes are **not** uploaded.
- **Practice** — morning / evening checklists and dedication text.

### What is the Daily Sutra?

A contextual recommendation with same-day cache. Optional **local** reminders.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **sutra lists**, **reading history**, **study progress**, **notes**, **chant preferences**, and **practice configuration** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site’s [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser’s address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796014949). Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。全部 **144** 条索引与内置经文均在本机。安装后，浏览、品读、诵经（系统 TTS）、学堂、念诵、功课、小组件与本地每日通知都**不需要网络**。抄经笔迹仅存本机。可选例外：打赏走 **Apple StoreKit**；以及你主动分享到其他 App。

### 和其他佛经 App 有什么不同？

爱佛经是**修习工作台**：中英对照 + 白话、段级高亮**诵经**、**持诵学堂**（背诵 / 填空 / 检视 / 测验 / 寻经）、**电子木鱼**、**抄经描红**（iOS）、**早晚功课**、**今日一经**、法卡、小组件、可选实时活动、Siri 与快捷——不是静态电子书。

### 收录了多少内容？

**144** 条索引（心经、金刚三十二分、阿弥陀经、药师十二愿、普门品、地藏十三品、密咒、坛经节选、四十二章经、八大人觉经等），另有 **101** 条法语。经文**仅供学习交流**。

### 「诵经」是什么意思？

指带**段级高亮**的朗读。可在设置里配置**播放内容矩阵**（原文 / 白话 / 英文），与屏幕显示解耦。

### 念诵、抄经、功课分别做什么？

- **念诵** — 本机木鱼计数（今日 / 累计 / 目标 / 音色），可选念诵实时活动。
- **抄经（iOS）** — PencilKit 描红，按句存稿，可导出 PNG / PDF；笔迹**不上传**。
- **功课** — 早 / 晚清单与回向文。

### 「今日一经」是什么？

情境推荐，当日缓存。可选**本机**提醒。

### iCloud 同步覆盖哪些内容？

可选同步：**收藏**、**经单**、**浏览记录**、**掌握度**、**笔记**、**念诵偏好**与**功课配置**——走你的私有 iCloud KVS。

### 会收集我的数据吗？

**不会。** 见 [隐私政策](privacy)：iHTC Studio 不收集数据；无广告/分析 SDK；无账号。

### App Store Connect 填哪条隐私政策 URL？

打开本站的 [隐私政策](privacy) 页面，从浏览器地址栏复制当前网址即可。

### 已经上架了吗？

已上架 — [App Store 下载](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796014949)。联系 [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) 获取支持。

### 系统要求？

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**，Mac **macOS 14.0+**。
