<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. All **512** chapters are bundled. After install, browsing, reading, recite (system TTS), study, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), and sharing to another app you choose.

### How is this different from a simple Analects reader?

iLunYu is a **studio**: **bilingual + vernacular reading**, karaoke-style **recite** with a playback content matrix, **Study classroom** (fade / fill-blank / meaning check / quiz / Hunt), **contextual Daily Verse**, share cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump of text.

### How many chapters are included?

**512** chapters across the twenty books of the *Analects*, with vernacular, English alignment, selected commentary excerpts, **40** famous lines, and **9** speakers who have attributed chapters.

### What does “recite” mean here?

Product term for read-aloud. Follow paragraph highlighting while listening. In Settings you can configure **what is spoken** (title / author / body × original / vernacular / English) independently from what you see on screen.

### What study modes are available?

**Fade**, **fill-blank**, **meaning check**, **quiz**, plus **Hunt (寻章)**. Mastery has levels with streaks. You can practice a single segment or a batch from favorites / reading lists.

### What is the Daily Verse?

A recommendation based on **solar terms**, **festivals**, and **seasons**, with same-day cache. Optional **local** reminders.

### Why is the Favorites tab missing?

Favorites is **off by default** (you can enable it in Settings → Tabs). You can still open favorites from Home.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **reading lists**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTCTeam; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site’s [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser’s address bar.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 13.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

<section lang="zh-CN" markdown="1">

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。全部 **512** 章内置。安装后，浏览、品鉴、诵读（系统 TTS）、学堂、小组件与本地每日通知都**不需要网络**。可选例外：打赏走 **Apple StoreKit**；以及你主动分享到其他 App。

### 和其他论语 App 有什么不同？

爱论语是**工作台**：**中英对照 + 白话**、可混读的**诵读**、**习礼学堂**（渐隐 / 填空 / 经义检测 / 测验 / 寻章）、**情境每日一则**、论卡、小组件、可选实时活动、Siri 与快捷——不是静态文本列表。

### 收录了多少章？

《论语》二十篇共 **512** 章，含白话、中英对照、精选集注摘录、**40** 条名句，以及 **9** 位有归章说话人。

### 「诵读」是什么？

即朗读跟读。听读时跟随段级高亮。设置里可单独配置**播放内容矩阵**（标题/作者/正文 × 原文/白话/英文），与屏幕显示语言解耦。

### 学堂有哪些模式？

**渐隐、填空、经义检测、测验**，另有**寻章**。掌握度分级配合打卡。可练本段，也可从收藏 / 读经单批量练习。

### 「每日一则」是什么？

结合**节气 / 节日 / 季节**的本地推荐（同日缓存）。可选**本机**提醒。

### 为什么看不到收藏 Tab？

收藏 Tab **默认关闭**（可在「设置 → Tab」打开）。仍可从首页进入收藏。

### iCloud 同步什么？

可选同步**收藏、读经单、浏览记录、掌握度与打卡**，走你的私有 iCloud。

### 会收集我的数据吗？

**不会。** 见 [隐私政策](privacy)：不收集数据；无广告/分析 SDK；无账号。

### App Store Connect 应填的隐私政策 URL？

打开本站 [隐私政策](privacy) 页，从浏览器地址栏复制网址。

### 系统要求？

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**，Mac **macOS 13.0+**。

### 如何联系支持？

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — 通常 48 小时内回复。
