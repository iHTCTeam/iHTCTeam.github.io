**One season, one passage — read the almanac of ideas.**

iLvShi (爱吕氏春秋 / 愛呂氏春秋) is an **offline Lüshi Chunqiu studio** — not another plain text dump. **No account required.** The *Lüshi Chunqiu* library ships on your device with **original / Gao You notes / vernacular / English (four layers)**. Start from **Daily Passage**. **Recite** with paragraph highlighting. Practice in **Spring–Autumn Study**. Play **Autumn Hunt**. Export **秋 cards**. Add **widgets**, optional **Reading Live Activity**, **Siri & Shortcuts**, and optional **iCloud**. Originals are public-domain study texts; notes are study aids. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

**At a glance:** 160 passages · ~1061 segments · 100 famous lines · Almanac (月令) · original / Gao You notes / vernacular / English (four layers) · recite · Spring–Autumn Study · Autumn Hunt · Daily Passage · 秋 cards · widgets · Control Center · `ilvshi://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started (step by step)

### 1. First launch

1. Install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6808957717) and open **iLvShi**.
2. Complete the welcome guide (daily reading → close reading → recite → study → system features → privacy).
3. Replay anytime: **Settings → Help → Feature Guide** (`ilvshi://feature-guide`).
4. Usage / Widget / Shortcuts guides live under **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s passage on Home

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. The **Daily Passage** hero shows today’s passage; tap to open detail.
3. Optional: enable local **Daily Passage notifications** in Settings.
4. Jump from Home into recite, Spring–Autumn Study, Autumn Hunt, favorites, and search.

### 3. Browse & search

1. Browse the full *Lüshi Chunqiu* corpus, famous lines, and specialty shelves.
2. Search titles and lines (Simplified / Traditional friendly). On iOS, optional **handwriting search** recognizes characters on device.
3. Star passages into **Favorites** and gather them into personal **秋单 lists**.

### 4. Close reading & share cards

1. Switch **bilingual / Chinese only / English only**; toggle layers independently where available.
2. Try immersive reading, vertical layout, font scale, and segmented pages for longer passages.
3. Open **秋 cards** → pick style / ratio → save to Photos (add-only) or system share.

### 5. Recite & Spring–Autumn Study

1. Open the recitation workbench for highlighted TTS, adjustable voice and pace, optional vernacular / English captions, continuous play, and background audio.
2. **Spring–Autumn Study** (`ilvshi://study`): fade, fill-blank, self-check, and quiz, with mastery tracking.
3. **Autumn Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game.

### 6. Widgets, Siri & system features

1. Add Daily / Famous Line / Favorites / Study Progress widgets (and Almanac where available).
2. Use Control Center, Siri, Spotlight, and `ilvshi://` deep links.
3. Optional Reading Live Activity stays available on supported iPhone devices.

### 7. Privacy & sync

1. Library, reading, recite (system TTS), study, widgets, and local notifications work **fully offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud Sync** mirrors favorites, lists, history, progress, streaks, and notes through **your private iCloud**.
3. Tip the developer (optional StoreKit) never unlocks a paywall and never creates an account.
