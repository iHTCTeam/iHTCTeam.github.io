<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Spring–Autumn Study, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

iLvShi is a **Lüshi Chunqiu studio**: close reading with **original / Gao You notes / vernacular / English (four layers)**, karaoke-style **recite**, **Spring–Autumn Study**, **Autumn Hunt**, **Daily Passage**, 秋 cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump. **Four-layer reading** with Gao You commentary; **This Month’s Almanac** widget; **5** widget kinds; **6** alternate icons.

### What texts are included?

*Lüshi Chunqiu*:

- 160 passages · ~1061 segments · 100 famous lines · Almanac (月令)
- Layers: original / Gao You notes / vernacular / English (four layers)

Originals are public-domain. Vernacular, English, and notes are curated study aids, not academic critical editions.

### What study modes are available?

**Fade**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, and **quiz**, with mastery tracking. **Autumn Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game.

### What is Daily Passage?

A daily passage on Home, optionally **contextual**, with optional **local** reminders. Widgets can keep it on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists (秋单 lists)**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6808957717).

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.
