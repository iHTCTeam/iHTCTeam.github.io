<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Legalism Study, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

iHanFei is a **Han Feizi studio**: close reading with **original / vernacular / English**, karaoke-style **recite**, **Legalism Study**, **Law Hunt**, **Daily Stratagem**, 法 cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump. **Fable gallery** and **fa–shu–shi concept map**; stamps 法/术/势/韓; **8** alternate icons; **6** widget kinds.

### What texts are included?

*Han Feizi*:

- 55 chapters · 5376 sentences · 225 famous lines · 85 fables · 47 concepts
- Layers: original / vernacular / English

Originals are public-domain. Vernacular, English, and notes are curated study aids, not academic critical editions.

### What study modes are available?

**Fade**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, and **quiz**, with mastery tracking. **Law Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game.

### What is Daily Stratagem?

A daily passage on Home, optionally **contextual**, with optional **local** reminders. Widgets can keep it on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists (简册 lists)**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6808957997).

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.
