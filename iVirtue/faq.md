<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. All **90** stanzas are bundled. After install, browsing, reading, recite (system TTS), study, practice streaks, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), and sharing to another app you choose.

### How is this different from a simple Di Zi Gui reader?

iVirtue is a **studio**: bilingual + vernacular reading, karaoke-style **recite**, **Virtue classroom** (recitation / fill-blank / self-check / quiz / Hunt), **daily practice tips & streaks**, share cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static ebook.

### How much content is included?

**90** stanzas across seven chapters, with vernacular and English, practice tips, selected life stories, and **28** famous lines.

### What does “recite” / “朗读” mean here?

Product term for read-aloud with **line-level highlighting**. In Settings you can configure **what is spoken** (title / author / body × original / vernacular / English) independently from what you see. Optional sleep timer stops playback at the set time.

### What study modes are available?

**Recitation**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, **quiz**, plus **Hunt (寻规)**. Mastery has levels. You can practice one stanza or a batch from favorites / rule lists (规单).

### What is Daily Rule + practice?

A daily recommendation with a **practice tip**. Check in on Home to build a streak. Optional **local** reminders.

### Font sizes for kids / elders?

Yes. In **Settings → Reading**, choose **standard / family / elder**. Body text (original & vernacular) follows this scale.

### Why isn’t there an “Authors” section?

Di Zi Gui is attributed to a single author in this library, so the Browse UI hides “by author” to avoid empty toggles.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **rule lists**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTCTeam; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site’s [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser’s address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796000304). Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 13.0+**.

<section lang="zh-CN" markdown="1">

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。全部 **90** 韵段内置。安装后，浏览、品读、朗读（系统 TTS）、学堂、践行打卡、小组件与本地每日通知都**不需要网络**。可选例外：打赏走 **Apple StoreKit**；以及你主动分享到其他 App。

### 和其他弟子规 App 有什么不同？

爱弟子规是**工作台**：中英对照 + 白话、句级高亮**朗读**、**美德学堂**（背诵 / 填空 / 自测 / 测验 / 寻规）、**每日践行提示与打卡**、分享卡、小组件、可选实时活动、Siri 与快捷——不是静态电子书。

### 收录了多少内容？

七篇章共 **90** 韵段，含白话与英译、践行提示、精选生活故事，以及 **28** 条名句。

### 「朗读」是什么？

即跟读朗读，带**句级高亮**。设置里可单独配置**播放内容矩阵**，与屏幕显示语言解耦。可选**睡眠定时**到点停播。

### 学堂有哪些模式？

**背诵、填空、自测、测验**，另有**寻规**。掌握度分级。可练单段，也可从收藏 / 规单批量练习。

### 「每日一规」和践行是什么？

每日推荐一条韵段，并附**践行提示**。在首页打卡积累连续天数。可选**本机**提醒。

### 有亲子 / 长辈字号吗？

有。在 **设置 → 阅读** 选择**标准 / 亲子 / 长辈**，原文与白话跟随此字号。

### 为什么没有「按作者」？

本库仅一位作者，分类界面会隐藏「按作者」，避免空开关。

### iCloud 同步什么？

可选同步**收藏、规单、浏览记录、掌握度与打卡**，走你的私有 iCloud。

### 会收集我的数据吗？

**不会。** 见 [隐私政策](privacy)。

### App Store Connect 应填的隐私政策 URL？

打开本站的 [隐私政策](privacy) 页面，从浏览器地址栏复制当前网址即可。

### 已经上架了吗？

是的 — [在 App Store 下载](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796000304)。支持问题可联系 [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com)。

### 系统要求？

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**，Mac **macOS 13.0+**。
