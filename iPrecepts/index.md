**One precept. A family way.**

iPrecepts (爱家训 / 愛家訓) is an **offline family-precept studio** — not another plain-text dump. **No account required.** All **118** works ship on your device: Zhu Xi and Zhu Bailu (the two Zhu family precepts), *Yan Zhitui’s Family Instructions*, *Admonitions to a Son*, Zeng Guofan’s household letters, *Yuan Family Maxims*, *Qian Family Precepts*, Kangxi’s *Court Instructions*, and later short admonitions. Every work carries **vernacular Chinese** and **English**. Unique **parallel compare** lets you read the same theme across generations. Start from **Daily Precept**. **Recite** with paragraph highlighting. Practice in **Family Study**. Export **precept cards**. Add **widgets**, optional **Reading Live Activity**, **Siri & Shortcuts**, and optional **iCloud**. Originals are public-domain; notes are study aids. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

**At a glance:** 118 precepts · 14 authors · 93 famous lines · bilingual · parallel compare · recite · Family Study · Precept Hunt · Daily Precept · precept cards · widgets · Control Center · `iprecepts://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started (step by step)

### 1. First launch

1. The App Store listing is being prepared. After release, install from the App Store and open **iPrecepts**.
2. Complete the welcome guide: Daily Precept → recitation → close reading → Family Study → parallel compare → gestures → privacy.
3. Replay anytime in **Settings → Help → Feature Guide** (`iprecepts://feature-guide`).
4. Usage, widget, and shortcuts guides live under **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s precept on Home

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. The **Daily Precept** hero shows today’s work; tap to open detail.
3. Optional: enable local **Daily Precept notifications** in Settings.
4. Jump from Home into recite, Family Study, Precept Hunt, favorites, and search.

### 3. Browse & search

1. Browse **Zhu Xi / Zhu Bailu**, **Yan Zhitui**, **Zhuge Liang**, **Zeng Guofan**, **Yuan Family Maxims**, **Qian Family Precepts**, **Court Instructions**, **later short admonitions**, themes, and **93 famous lines**.
2. Search titles, source text, and famous lines. On iOS, optional **handwriting search** recognizes characters on device.
3. Star works into **Favorites** and gather them into personal **lists** (训单).

### 4. Close reading, compare & precept cards

1. Switch **bilingual / Chinese only / English only**; toggle vernacular, English, and pinyin independently.
2. Open **parallel compare** (`iprecepts://compare`) to read the same household theme across dynasties.
3. Try immersive reading, vertical layout, centering, font scale, and segmented pages for longer passages.
4. Open **Precept Card** → pick style / ratio → save to Photos (add-only) or system share.

### 5. Recite & Family Study

1. Open the recitation workbench for highlighted TTS, adjustable voice and pace, optional vernacular / English captions, continuous play, and background audio.
2. **Family Study** (`iprecepts://study`): fade, fill-blank, self-check, and quiz, with mastery tracking.
3. **Precept Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-precept game.

### 6. Widgets, Siri & system features

1. Add **Daily Precept / Famous Line / Favorites / Study Progress** widgets.
2. Use Control Center, Siri, Spotlight, and `iprecepts://` deep links.
3. Optional Reading Live Activity stays available on supported iPhone devices.

### 7. Privacy & sync

1. Library, reading, recite (system TTS), Family Study, widgets, and local notifications work **fully offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud Sync** mirrors favorites, lists, history, progress, streaks, and notes through **your private iCloud**.
3. Tip the developer (optional StoreKit) never unlocks a paywall and never creates an account.

## Why you’ll want to download

- **A family way you can reread** — not only translation, but precepts that still speak to household, learning, and character.
- **Twin Zhu plus later lineages** — Zhu Xi, Zhu Bailu, Yan Zhitui, Zeng Guofan, Qian, Kangxi and more, offline.
- **Cross-generation compare** — put the same theme side by side instead of reading one book in isolation.
- **Privacy by design** — no account, no ads, no analytics SDK.
