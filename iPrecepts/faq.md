<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library, vernacular, English, and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Family Study, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

iPrecepts is a **family-precept studio**: bilingual close reading, **parallel compare** across generations, karaoke-style **recite**, **Family Study**, **Precept Hunt**, **Daily Precept**, precept cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump of text.

### What texts are included?

**118** works of family instruction, including:

- Zhu Xi, *Family Precepts*, and Zhu Bailu, *Household Maxims* (the two Zhu precepts)
- Yan Zhitui, *Family Instructions* (20 chapters)
- Zhuge Liang, *Admonitions to a Son*
- Zeng Guofan household letters
- Yuan Cai, *Yuan Family Maxims*
- *Qian Family Precepts*; Kangxi, *Court Instructions*; later short admonitions

**14** authors · **93** famous lines.

Originals are public-domain. Vernacular, English, and notes are curated study aids, not academic editions.

### What is parallel compare?

A side-by-side reading of the **same household theme** (learning, frugality, speech, kinship) across dynasties — so Zhu Xi, Yan Zhitui, and Zeng Guofan can answer one question together. Open via `iprecepts://compare`.


### What study modes are available?

**Fade**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, and **quiz**, with mastery tracking. **Precept Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game.

### What is Daily Precept?

A daily passage on Home, optionally **contextual**, with optional **local** reminders. Widgets can keep it on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists (训单)**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Not yet — the listing is being prepared. We will add an App Store link on this page when it goes live. Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。语料、白话、英译与学习辅助均内置。安装后浏览、对照品读、诵读（系统 TTS）、家训学堂、小组件与每日通知均可**离线**使用。可选例外：**StoreKit 打赏**（Apple）、分享到你选择的其他 App，以及可选 iCloud。

### 和普通电子书有什么不同？

爱家训是**家训工作台**：中英对照、**跨代对照**、卡拉 OK 式**诵读**、**家训学堂**、**寻训**、**今日一训**、训卡、小组件、可选实时活动、Siri 与快捷指令——不是静态原文。

### 收录了哪些内容？

**118** 篇家训，包括：

- 朱熹《紫阳朱子家训》与朱柏庐《治家格言》（朱子双璧）
- 颜之推《颜氏家训》二十篇
- 诸葛亮《诫子书》等
- 曾国藩家训
- 袁采《袁氏世范》
- 《钱氏家训》、康熙《庭训格言》与历代诫子短篇

**14** 位作者 · **93** 条金句。

原文为公版典籍。白话、英译与注解为学习参考，并非学术校勘本。

### 「跨代对照」是什么？

把同一治家主题（劝学、勤俭、言语、亲亲）在不同朝代并排阅读——让朱子、颜之推与曾国藩同答一问。入口：`iprecepts://compare`。


### 有哪些学堂模式？

**渐隐**、**填空**、**默写**与**测验**，并记录掌握度。首页 **寻训** 是独立的找篇小游戏。

### 「今日一训」是什么？

首页每日一篇，可按情境挑选，并可开启**本地**提醒。小组件可把它留在主屏幕、锁定屏幕与待机。

### iCloud 同步包含什么？

可选同步 **收藏**、**训单**、**笔记**、**阅读历史**、**学堂进度**与**打卡**，走你的私有 iCloud 键值存储。

### 你们收集我的数据吗？

**不收集。** 详见[隐私政策](privacy)。

打开本站 [隐私政策](privacy) 页，从浏览器地址栏复制当前网址。
