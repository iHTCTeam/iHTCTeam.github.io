**One Mencius saying — walk with expansive spirit.**

iMencius (爱孟子 / 愛孟子) is an **offline Mencius studio** — not another plain text dump. **No account required.** The *Mencius* library ships on your device with **original / vernacular / Legge English**. Start from **Daily Saying**. **Recite** with paragraph highlighting. Practice in **Nurturing Qi Study**. Play **Yi Hunt**. Export **孟 cards**. Add **widgets**, optional **Reading Live Activity**, **Siri & Shortcuts**, and optional **iCloud**. Originals are public-domain study texts; notes are study aids. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

**At a glance:** 690 chapters · 3549 verses · 100 famous lines · 26 analogies · 7 dialogue voices · original / vernacular / Legge English · recite · Nurturing Qi Study · Yi Hunt · Daily Saying · 孟 cards · widgets · Control Center · `imencius://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started (step by step)

### 1. First launch

1. Install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6808957787) and open **iMencius**.
2. Complete the welcome guide (daily reading → close reading → recite → study → system features → privacy).
3. Replay anytime: **Settings → Help → Feature Guide** (`imencius://feature-guide`).
4. Usage / Widget / Shortcuts guides live under **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s passage on Home

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. The **Daily Saying** hero shows today’s passage; tap to open detail.
3. Optional: enable local **Daily Saying notifications** in Settings.
4. Jump from Home into recite, Nurturing Qi Study, Yi Hunt, favorites, and search.

### 3. Browse & search

1. Browse the full *Mencius* corpus, famous lines, and specialty shelves.
2. Search titles and lines (Simplified / Traditional friendly). On iOS, optional **handwriting search** recognizes characters on device.
3. Star passages into **Favorites** and gather them into personal **读孟单 lists**.

### 4. Close reading & share cards

1. Switch **bilingual / Chinese only / English only**; toggle layers independently where available.
2. Try immersive reading, vertical layout, font scale, and segmented pages for longer passages.
3. Open **孟 cards** → pick style / ratio → save to Photos (add-only) or system share.

### 5. Recite & Nurturing Qi Study

1. Open the recitation workbench for highlighted TTS, adjustable voice and pace, optional vernacular / English captions, continuous play, and background audio.
2. **Nurturing Qi Study** (`imencius://study`): fade, fill-blank, self-check, and quiz, with mastery tracking.
3. **Yi Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game.

### 6. Widgets, Siri & system features

1. Add Daily / Famous Line / Favorites / Study Progress widgets (and Almanac where available).
2. Use Control Center, Siri, Spotlight, and `imencius://` deep links.
3. Optional Reading Live Activity stays available on supported iPhone devices.

### 7. Privacy & sync

1. Library, reading, recite (system TTS), study, widgets, and local notifications work **fully offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud Sync** mirrors favorites, lists, history, progress, streaks, and notes through **your private iCloud**.
3. Tip the developer (optional StoreKit) never unlocks a paywall and never creates an account.

## 开始使用（逐步）

### 1. 首次启动

1. 从 [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6808957787) 安装并打开 **爱孟子**。
2. 完成欢迎引导（每日一文 → 对照品读 → 诵读 → 学堂 → 系统能力 → 隐私）。

### 2. 在首页读今日内容

1. 打开**首页**。
2. **每日一语** 主卡展示今日篇目；点按进入详情。
3. 可选：在设置中开启本地 **每日一语 通知**。
4. 从首页进入诵读、养气学堂、寻义、收藏与搜索。

### 3. 浏览与搜索

1. 浏览全本《孟子》、名句与专属书架。
2. 搜索标题与文句（简繁友好）。在 iOS 上可选**手写寻句**，本机识别。
3. 收藏篇目，并整理到个人 **读孟单**。

### 4. 对照品读与分享卡

1. 切换**对照 / 仅中文 / 仅英文**；按需独立开关各层。
2. 尝试沉浸阅读、竖排、字号与分段翻页。
3. 打开 **孟卡** → 选风格 / 比例 → 保存到照片（仅添加）或系统分享。

### 5. 诵读与养气学堂

1. 打开诵读工作台：高亮 TTS、语速音色、可选白话／英文字幕、连播与后台播放。
2. **养气学堂**（`imencius://study`）：渐隐、填空、自测与测验，并记录掌握度。
3. 首页 **寻义** 是独立的寻句游戏。

### 6. 小组件、Siri 与系统能力

1. 添加每日／名句／收藏／进度等小组件（月令类应用另有月令组件）。
2. 使用控制中心、Siri、Spotlight 与 `imencius://` 深链。
3. 支持机型可开启阅读实时活动。

### 7. 隐私与同步

1. 语料、品读、诵读（系统 TTS）、学堂、小组件与本地通知安装后均可**完全离线**。
2. 可选 **iCloud 同步** 通过**你的私人 iCloud** 同步收藏、清单、历史、进度、打卡与笔记。
3. 打赏开发者（可选 StoreKit）不解锁付费墙，也不创建账号。
