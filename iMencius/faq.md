<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Nurturing Qi Study, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

iMencius is a **Mencius studio**: close reading with **original / vernacular / Legge English**, karaoke-style **recite**, **Nurturing Qi Study**, **Yi Hunt**, **Daily Saying**, 孟 cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump. **Analogy gallery** and **dialogue map**; Legge public-domain English; **4** widget kinds; **6** alternate icons.

### What texts are included?

*Mencius*:

- 690 chapters · 3549 verses · 100 famous lines · 26 analogies · 7 dialogue voices
- Layers: original / vernacular / Legge English

Originals are public-domain. Vernacular, English, and notes are curated study aids, not academic critical editions.

### What study modes are available?

**Fade**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, and **quiz**, with mastery tracking. **Yi Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game.

### What is Daily Saying?

A daily passage on Home, optionally **contextual**, with optional **local** reminders. Widgets can keep it on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists (读孟单 lists)**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6808957787).

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.
